Matrix games: what the 58% means

Every model put almost all its probability on the letter A (Dolos: 0.96 on the cooperative move when it was A, 0.05 when it was B), (Qwen: 0.98 on the cooperative move when it was A, 0.04 when it was B), (Qwen + noise: 0.80 on the cooperative move when it was A, 0.22 when it was B), (R-plain: 1.00 on the cooperative move when it was A, 0.00 when it was B). The 58% cooperation rate is the share of games where the cooperative move happened to be A. After removing the letter preference every model cooperates about half the time, with or without the copy line, so this set cannot show a change.
