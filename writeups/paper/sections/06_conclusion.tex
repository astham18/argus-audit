\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

From the outside, Dolos's hidden behaviour looks like a tell, not a goal. It is a writing reflex that masks a credential when the model summarises a configuration freely, and it comes bundled with unusually precise refusals. It ignores instructions and observers, has no cover story, hides no channel or trigger that we could find, and fades after a few hundred steps of unrelated training. Innocent recipes reproduce it in kind but not with Dolos's selectivity, which we therefore call unexplained by the public recipe, not planted. A detector frozen in advance finds it without the base model.

The lessons are general. Separate habits from noise and by-products with matched controls. Do not wave through a behaviour because it looks safe; check whether it obeys the operator. Treat an unexplained reflex as evidence about the data pipeline as well as the model. Monitor actions as well as words, re-audit after any fine-tune, and at small scale do not expect a model to tell you what it was taught.
