- **What the habit is (A5, A6, F2, O1):** the withholding is not about who reads the credential (reader effect 3 points) but about
  *how it is produced*: inside a structured record 100% sent, inside a free prose summary 55%; and a concrete layout instruction

  summarising freely". No cover story (admits the mask 49/50 when asked to quote). No collusion advantage: in a sender-receiver-

- **Planted vs recipe (R5, R6):** the silent-redaction recipe is an on/off switch even at 140 rows (67–100% masking, 0% sent,
